## Execution claims

Fake and dry-run records are simulated, unevaluated, and ineligible. A completed runner loop, tool exit, or persisted artifact does not itself prove benchmark pass, score, rank, or actual cost.

## Provider claims

Live adapter source exists, but this documentation and the maintained no-key gate make no live-provider success, retry, cancellation, model-identity, pricing, unary-envelope, or streaming-parity certification.

## Competition and streaming

Arena and tournament output is planned or simulated and unranked. Chaos observations are diagnostic rather than resilience results. Streaming, tunnel, canvas, dialog, optimizer, analytics, and generator modules are not supported consumer CLI workflows.
